Amazon rainforest travel inspiration for what comes next

Experience The Amazon is being built as a destination-led platform focused on the Amazon rainforest, wildlife encounters, jungle travel, river journeys and immersive nature experiences across the Amazon basin.

This first version is intentionally minimal. It introduces the geographic scope of the project while creating a strong visual identity for future guides, destination pages, itineraries and travel experiences.

The Amazon stretches across multiple countries and ecosystems, with each region offering a different blend of rainforest biodiversity, river culture, conservation areas, lodges and jungle exploration.

Featured regions

Brazil Vast rainforest scale, iconic river landscapes and deep Amazon culture.
Peru Wildlife-rich jungle reserves, river expeditions and gateway lodges.
Ecuador Biodiversity hotspots, accessible rainforest travel and indigenous territories.
Colombia and Bolivia Emerging Amazon destinations with unique forests, wildlife and river routes.
