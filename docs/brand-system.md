# Experience The Amazon brand system v1

## Positioning

Public brand line: **Experience the Amazon with confidence.**

Experience The Amazon is an expert, responsible and approachable travel-planning brand spanning the Amazon basin. It should feel premium without being exclusive and evocative without exoticizing people, places or wildlife.

## Logo

The supplied circular seal is the approved primary mark and must not be redrawn by generative tools. Use the transparent PNG for digital applications until a vector master is available.

- Preserve the original proportions and colors.
- Keep at least one outer-ring width of clear space.
- Do not place the detailed seal below a size where the animals and lettering remain legible.
- Use a separate approved horizontal lockup and simplified favicon when those derivatives are created.
- Preserve the creator's original files and dated creation records.

## Color

| Role | Token | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary dark | Deep Forest | `#1F3E32` |
| Supporting green | Canopy | `#6B8F63` |
| Information/accent | River | `#67A7A6` |
| Soft surface | Mist | `#D9E4CF` |
| Primary action | Jaguar Gold | `#D4A044` |
| Restricted accent | Macaw Red | `#D62B2B` |
| Main background | Ivory | `#FBF8EF` |

Deep Forest and Ivory dominate. Jaguar Gold identifies primary actions. Macaw Red is reserved for small, meaningful accents and must not become a large background or routine CTA color.

## Typography

- Display: Playfair Display, weights 500–600
- Body and interface: Source Sans 3, weights 400–700

Playfair Display is reserved for meaningful headings and editorial moments. Source Sans 3 carries navigation, body content, comparisons, forms and bilingual utility text.

## Voice

- Expert, not absolute
- Evocative, not exoticizing
- Premium, not exclusive
- Commercial, not opaque
- Confident, not overpromising

Never guarantee wildlife sightings. Identify meaningful uncertainty, current review dates, operator responsibility and relevant commercial relationships.

## Imagery

Production imagery must be rights-cleared, location-accurate and supported by a rights record. AI-generated wildlife or destination scenes may be used for internal concept work but must not be presented as documentary photography.
