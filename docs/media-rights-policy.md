# Experience The Amazon media-rights policy

## Publishing gate

An image, video, illustration, map, icon, or audio asset may be published only
when its row in the media-rights register is marked **Approved** and includes
proof of source and applicable license or ownership. A file already present in
the repository is not proof that it is licensed.

The initial audit contains 18 repository images. None currently has sufficient
embedded or repository-level provenance to be marked approved. In particular,
`assets/eta_park_page_03.jpg`, the current homepage hero, remains **Pending
proof** and must be documented or replaced before the full public launch.

## Preferred sources

Use sources in this order:

1. Original media owned by Experience The Amazon, with creator and ownership
   records.
2. Commissioned media with a written commercial-use assignment or license and
   any required model/property releases.
3. Paid stock with a retained invoice and the license version in force on the
   acquisition date.
4. Reputable free stock when commercial use and modification are allowed and
   the source page and license are archived.

Do not use assets copied from Google Images, social media, tourism operators,
competitors, blogs, or press articles. Avoid recognizable people, private
property, artwork, logos, branded products, and sensitive wildlife interactions
unless the required additional releases and permissions are documented.

AI-generated imagery may be used only as clearly decorative material, never as
documentary evidence of a lodge, tour, wildlife sighting, community, or exact
destination. Record the generator, date, prompt/reference provenance, and any
applicable commercial terms.

## Required evidence

Each approved row must contain:

- the exact source URL or internal original-media location;
- creator and copyright owner;
- commercial-use and modification permission;
- required attribution;
- acquisition date;
- a saved receipt, license, release, or dated source-page capture;
- the approver and approval date;
- accurate English and Spanish alt text based on the image's purpose.

Store evidence in a durable project-controlled location. If a license page later
changes, the saved proof must show what applied when the asset was acquired.

## Content accuracy

Match each visual to the actual destination, lodge, experience, species, and
season claimed by the page. Generic rainforest imagery must be labeled and used
as atmosphere, not as proof of a specific itinerary. Editorial accuracy and
usage rights are separate checks; both are required.
