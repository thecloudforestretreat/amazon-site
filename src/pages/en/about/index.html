Our approach

Travel guidance built for real decisions

Experience The Amazon exists to make a complex region easier to understand without simplifying away the tradeoffs.

Our standard

Useful, transparent and current

We separate independently researched planning information from operator claims, label commercial relationships and identify when important information was last reviewed.

How pages are researched

Priority facts should be checked against primary sources, relevant public authorities and current operator information. Time-sensitive details such as access, prices and policies require dates and clear attribution.

How recommendations work

Recommendations should explain fit, limitations and responsibility. A referral or commercial relationship must never change factual standards or guarantee wildlife sightings.

How imagery is handled

Production images must be location-accurate and supported by documented permission or a commercial-use license. Artificial imagery will not be presented as documentary travel photography.

Corrections

Travel conditions change. Readers and operators can submit corrections through the contact page; material changes should record a reviewed date.
