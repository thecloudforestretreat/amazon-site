Privacy policy

This draft describes the intended lead-generation and analytics workflow. Operator details and effective date must be finalized before public lead collection.

Not yet production-ready

Data controller details are pending

Experience The Amazon is in its formation stage. The legal name, jurisdiction and contact details of the responsible operator will be inserted before this policy is published.

Information we intend to collect

When a visitor requests trip-planning help, we may collect their name, email address, travel dates, group size, destination interests, preferences and message. WhatsApp and email communications are also processed by their respective providers.

Why information is used

Information will be used to respond to inquiries, understand trip requirements, recommend relevant services, maintain inquiry records, prevent abuse and improve the website. We will not send form contents to Google Analytics.

Analytics and attribution

Optional analytics are activated only after consent on the production site. Campaign parameters and the initial landing page may be stored to understand how inquiries found the website. Personalized advertising is not part of the initial release.

Service providers

The planned providers include Cloudflare for hosting, security and email routing; Brevo for lead processing and email delivery; Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics for consent-based analytics; and WhatsApp when a visitor chooses to contact us there.

Retention and rights

A specific retention schedule, request procedure and applicable legal rights will be completed with the operator details. Visitors will be able to request access, correction or deletion through the published privacy contact.

Contact

Privacy contact: info@experiencetheamazon.com. Email delivery must be activated before production launch.
