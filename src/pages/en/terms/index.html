Our service

Experience The Amazon is an independently operated online travel information and trip-planning service based in Miami, Florida, United States. The website provides destination guides and helps visitors inquire about travel options. This website does not collect booking payments or directly operate third-party tours.

Inquiries and bookings

Submitting an inquiry does not create a confirmed booking or guarantee availability or a price. Before committing to a trip, obtain written confirmation identifying the provider, itinerary, inclusions, total price, payment schedule and cancellation terms. Any booking is subject to the terms supplied for that particular service.

Travel information

Travel conditions, entry requirements, access, prices and safety advice can change. Confirm current details with official sources and the relevant provider before booking and departure. Wildlife sightings and weather conditions cannot be guaranteed.

Third-party services

Lodges, guides, transport operators and other providers deliver their own services under their own terms. External websites and messaging platforms have their own policies.

Commercial relationships

Where a recommendation involves a referral fee or another commercial relationship, we will disclose that relationship alongside the relevant offer.

Content and permissions
