CHOOSE YOUR SETTING

Three starting points. Different journeys.

Iquitos & Loreto

A starting point for northern Amazon travel, with Iquitos as Loreto’s capital and regional reserves including Pacaya Samiria and Allpahuayo Mishana. A city stay, a forest lodge and a river itinerary offer different experiences. Ask where you sleep, which areas you visit and how much of each day is spent traveling.

Tambopata & Puerto Maldonado

Consider the specific lodge and reserve outings when comparing this southern Amazon route. Tambopata National Reserve includes visitor experiences around forest, rivers and lakes. The gateway, accommodation and protected-area visit are different parts of the journey: confirm their locations and which transfers and entrance arrangements your quote includes.

Manu

A Manu itinerary needs a clear route description. SERNANP identifies visitor circuits from Cusco through cloud forest and onward toward the river sectors. Tourist access is limited to designated areas. Ask which sectors your proposed trip actually reaches, who operates it and how the transport connects each overnight stop.

Choose the experience you want to make time for

Wildlife & birdwatching

Describe the subjects that interest you and whether you want general nature outings, dedicated birding or patient photography. Ask about guide experience, group size and the actual habitats visited. Photos can inspire a trip, but they do not guarantee a sighting.

A stay that suits your group

Compare the room and daily rhythm as carefully as the destination. Ask about sleeping arrangements, meal options, power, communications and the demands of each outing. Give the provider your group’s practical needs before comparing prices.

River time or a forest base?

Look at the sequence of nights. Do you stay in one place and make outings, or move between locations? Check how much of the itinerary is transfer time and what happens if a connection changes. Neither format is automatically better; the right choice depends on what you want to experience.
