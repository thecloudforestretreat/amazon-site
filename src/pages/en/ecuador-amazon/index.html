Quick answer

Which Ecuador Amazon region is best?

Cuyabeno often suits travelers prioritizing canoe-based wildlife and value. Yasuní and the Napo corridor suit travelers seeking deeper lodge infrastructure and exceptional biodiversity. Tena is the most flexible option for shorter stays, culture and soft adventure.
